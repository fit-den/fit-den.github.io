About FIT DEN

More than a place to work out.

FIT DEN is a dedicated training space in Amghata for beginners, fitness enthusiasts and strength-focused athletes. Your fitness, your goals, your den.

  • Large, bright training hall with a clean floor and full-length mirrors
  • Wide equipment variety, so you spend less time waiting
  • Supportive trainers and members, great for beginners
  • Strength, powerlifting, cardio and nutrition guidance under one roof
